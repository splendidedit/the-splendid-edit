The Season / New York

Veronica Leoni leaves Calvin Klein after four seasons.

By The Splendid Edit October 2026 Fashion
← Back to The Edit

Calvin Klein confirmed the split on the fifteenth of October. Veronica Leoni will leave when her contract ends, and her Spring 2027 show in September becomes the last word of a four-season run. The house has not named who follows.

Calvin Klein put the news in plain terms. Leoni and the house will end their collaboration at the close of her current contract. The company thanked her for her creative leadership and said Collection stays part of its long-term plan. It added that it would share what comes next later, which leaves the timing, the shape and the ownership of the next chapter open.

This reads as a leadership change, not a withdrawal from the runway. Calvin Klein has kept the distinction clear. Collection is still on the table. The author of its next season is not.

Four seasons

Leoni joined in May 2024. She was the first woman to lead the Calvin Klein design studio, and her Fall 2025 debut brought Collection back to New York Fashion Week after a seven-year absence from the schedule. The label had become a name on stores and campaigns. She made it a runway again.

Across four collections she built a spare American wardrobe. Coats, shirts, tailoring, slips. She worked in proportion rather than novelty, and let a white shirt or a long coat carry the room. Her final Spring 2027 show, staged inside Terminal Warehouse, an 1891 brick block near the Hudson, took that logic to its end. Tailoring came in cotton and light wool. Evening arrived on the bias, so the fabric moved instead of holding a shape. Denim, leather and silk-knit tees widened the sportswear vocabulary without raising the volume.

Colour did the punctuation. A base of black and off-whites, then scarlet, royal blue and a pale citron cutting through it. The house named one check after Georgia O'Keeffe's own wardrobe and let the reference sit without overworking it. Accessories kept pace, from elongated scarves to a new triangular bag shape built to carry into the next year.

Four seasons is a short tenure by the measure of a heritage house. It was long enough to set a grammar. Anyone who follows will be read against it.

The parent

Calvin Klein sits inside PVH, and the exit arrives against a softer quarter. In results dated the second of September, PVH reported Calvin Klein revenue down seven percent, with owned digital commerce the brighter line. A runway settles none of that. It gives a global business one clear image to work from, which is why a company this size keeps staging one.

Leoni's value to PVH ran past the clothes shown in September. Collection works as a laboratory for silhouette, casting and image, and the pictures it generates travel into stores and campaigns through the year. Whoever takes the role inherits that reach, not only a show.

The opening

No replacement has been named, so any list of candidates is guesswork. What the announcement leaves out carries as much weight as what it states. It does not confirm whether Calvin Klein will appoint a single creative director, divide the role, or change the runway cadence. It does not set a date for the next Collection presentation.

The market for talent is busy. Seán McGirr left Alexander McQueen this autumn, and other houses have moved their creative leadership in the same stretch. A designer's arrival or exit can become the loudest story of a season. New York now has one of its most watched seats open, and the eventual name will say how the house wants to be read.

The gap also tests how Calvin Klein reads its own recent history. Seven years off the schedule taught the business that a show is a tool, not a given. Bringing one back was Leoni's clearest contribution. Keeping it credible under a new hand is the work that outlasts her.

The reading

It would be early to call this a verdict on minimalism. The record shows a personnel change and a house that insists the line still matters. Leoni leaves a clear template behind. A coat, a shirt, a slip, each able to move from runway to window to campaign without a new story at every step. That legibility is the hardest thing to build and the easiest to keep.

Her last collection still has to reach the floor while Calvin Klein prepares an audience for a voice it has not introduced. The clothes for Spring 2027 are finished. The decision about what Calvin Klein sounds like next is the one left on the table.

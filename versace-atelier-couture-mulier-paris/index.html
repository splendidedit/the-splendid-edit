Versace takes its atelier back to Paris couture

By The Splendid Edit 30 October 2026 Fashion
← Back to The Edit

Versace will show couture in Paris again. The house confirmed in late October that Atelier Versace returns to the haute couture runway in January 2027, with Pieter Mulier presenting his first couture collection. It is the first dedicated Atelier show since 2016.

The statement arrived on 28 October and set the stage inside Paris Couture Week, which runs 25 to 28 January 2027. The exact date and venue are still to come. The marker itself is the point. A house that spent a decade dressing couture through private orders and red carpets is putting a collection back on a runway.

The timing says as much as the clothes will. Versace now belongs to the Prada Group, which closed its acquisition this year in a deal valued at close to 1.4 billion dollars. Couture is the loudest way a new owner can state intent. You do not reopen an atelier to sell evening gowns by the dozen. You do it to set the register for everything underneath.

The return

Gianni Versace launched Atelier Versace and showed it in Paris in 1990. The name stood for the hand work, the beading and the draping that his ready-to-wear borrowed from. Donatella Versace carried it forward for almost three decades and showed the last dedicated couture runway in July 2016. She stepped back from the design lead in 2025.

What filled the gap was a quieter kind of couture. Versace kept dressing the Croisette, the Oscars and the Met through made-to-order work, visible one gown at a time rather than as a full collection. Nicole Kidman wore a black lace Atelier Versace gown to the Los Angeles premiere of Practical Magic 2 on 31 August. The craft never left. The show did.

The man

Pieter Mulier is Belgian and exact. Versace named him chief creative officer on 5 February, and he took the role on 1 July. He arrived from Alaia, where he spent five years and rebuilt the house around cut, volume and a small vocabulary of shapes worked season after season.

Before Alaia he worked alongside Raf Simons for years, at Jil Sander, at Dior and at Calvin Klein. That lineage reads as the opposite of the Versace cliche. One house is sun, gold and the body on display; the other is line, proportion and the quiet drama of a seam. The interest sits in the collision.

The gap

Mulier is the second designer to lead Versace under the new structure. Dario Vitale came first, appointed early and gone after a single season once the acquisition settled. His brief chapter left the house without a clear creative voice at the exact moment it changed hands. Mulier inherits that pause.

A couture show answers it directly. Ready-to-wear takes months to reach a store and longer to reach a verdict. A runway of hand-made clothes lands in one evening, in one room, in front of the people who decide what a house means next season. January gives Mulier that room before his commercial collections have to carry the weight.

The stakes

Couture is small and expensive and watched by everyone. A handful of clients buy it. Millions read it. For a house in transition it works as a thesis statement, the place where a designer shows the full reach of what the ateliers can do before the ideas get translated down into bags and ready-to-wear.

Versace has the material to work with. The Medusa, the baroque prints, the chainmail, the Greek key, the vault of Gianni's archive. The question is how much Mulier keeps and how much he sets aside. His Alaia years suggest he will cut rather than pile on, and that a Versace under his hand may trade some of the volume for precision.

The read

Paris in January is already crowded with houses testing new creative directors. Versace enters that week as the sharpest test of the season, because it carries the most recognizable codes in fashion and the least settled recent history. A debut here is read against Gianni, against Donatella, and against every Versace moment the audience already holds in memory.

Mulier has done a version of this before. He walked into Alaia after Azzedine Alaia and made the house his own without erasing it. Versace is louder, bigger and more scrutinized, and the brief is the same. Honor the founder, serve the owner, and put something on the runway that could only come from this address.

The date to watch falls in the last week of January. One collection, one evening, and the first real answer to what Versace becomes in the Prada era. The atelier opens its doors again, and the whole of fashion week will be in the room to see what walks out.
